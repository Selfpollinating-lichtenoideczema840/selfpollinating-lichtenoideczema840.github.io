### My Music Won't Play
- Check your internet connection

- Restart Nativune

### The App Won't Open
- Right-click the Nativune icon and choose "Run as administrator"
- Disable any security software temporarily to see if it's blocking the app
- Restart your computer and try again

### Audio Quality Issues
- Check your system volume settings
- Ensure your speakers or headphones are properly connected
- Try closing other apps that might be using audio
